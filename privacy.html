DarAtiq Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

DarAtiq is a small online shop based in Morocco that sells printable wall art, planners and printable party games through Etsy (shop: DarAtiq, at https://www.etsy.com/shop/DarAtiq). This privacy policy explains how the DarAtiq Pinterest application ("the App") handles information.

What the App does

The App connects to the Pinterest API using OAuth 2.0 so that the owner of DarAtiq can publish Pins, create and manage boards, and read account statistics on DarAtiq's own Pinterest business account. The App is used by the shop owner only, on the owner's own account. It is not offered to other users, and it does not collect data about other Pinterest users.

Information the App accesses

Through the Pinterest API, the App accesses only data belonging to the DarAtiq Pinterest account itself: the account profile, its boards and its Pins, including basic performance statistics. The App does not access Pinterest data belonging to any other person or business.

How the information is used

The information is used solely to run DarAtiq's own Pinterest presence: posting Pins that link to DarAtiq products, organising them into boards, and checking how those Pins perform so the shop can improve its content.

Sharing

DarAtiq does not sell, rent or share any data obtained through the Pinterest API with third parties. Product images and links published as Pins are public marketing content posted by the shop itself.

Storage and security

API access tokens are stored securely by the automation platform used by the shop owner and are never published or shared. Access can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account's connected-apps settings, which immediately stops the App from accessing the account.

Your choices

Because the App only operates the owner's own account, no third-party personal data is processed. Anyone who interacts with DarAtiq Pins on Pinterest is covered by Pinterest's own privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address with a new "Last updated" date.
